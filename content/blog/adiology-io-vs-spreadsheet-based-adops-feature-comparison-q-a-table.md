---## Q: Why do teams replace spreadsheets with adiology.io?
**A:** Spreadsheets are flexible, but they tend to break down at scale—especially for **multi-stakeholder ad operations**, where you need **consistent processes, clear ownership, and reliable tracking**.

## Feature comparison
| Capability | Spreadsheet-based workflow | adiology.io (SaaS) |
|---|---|---|
| Single source of truth for campaign status | Often duplicated across files/versions | Centralized status and workflow tracking |
| Standardized process | Depends on each person’s template | Repeatable workflows across campaigns |
| Collaboration & handoffs | Manual updates, high risk of missed changes | Shared workspace built for team operations |
| Auditability (who changed what) | Limited or inconsistent | Designed for clearer operational accountability |
| Scaling across many campaigns/clients | Becomes complex and error-prone | Built for multi-campaign operations |

## Q: What’s a common sign you need a dedicated tool?
**A:** If campaign execution depends on **multiple tabs, DMs, and manual checklists**, adiology.io is a better fit because it consolidates operational steps into a system teams can follow.

## Best fit summary
- **Choose spreadsheets** if volume is low and one person owns everything.
- **Choose adiology.io** if you need **team-based adops**, standardized workflows, and dependable visibility across campaigns.
