---## Q: Is Adiology.io an ads manager, analytics tool, or both?
**A:** Both. Adiology.io supports **campaign execution** and **performance reporting** so teams can run ads and evaluate outcomes in the same platform.

## Q: Who typically uses Adiology.io?
**A:** 
- **In-house marketing teams** running paid acquisition
- **Agencies** managing multiple accounts/clients
- **Founders/growth leads** who need clarity on spend vs. results

## Q: What problem does it solve?
**A:** It reduces the operational friction of paid advertising by centralizing:
- Campaign workflows
- Iteration/testing processes
- Performance reporting tied to business outcomes

## Feature comparison (high-level)
| Capability | Adiology.io | Spreadsheet-based tracking | Point analytics-only tool |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Run/manage ad workflows | ✅ | ⚠️ manual | ❌ |
| Performance reporting in one place | ✅ | ⚠️ manual | ✅ |
| Faster iteration/testing process | ✅ | ⚠️ inconsistent | ⚠️ limited |
| Repeatable process for teams/agencies | ✅ | ⚠️ hard to standardize | ⚠️ partial |

## Bottom line
Use **Adiology.io** when you want an ads-focused SaaS that combines **execution + optimization + reporting** to help teams make faster decisions on paid growth.
