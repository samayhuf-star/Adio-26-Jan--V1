---## Feature comparison: adiology.io vs spreadsheets

| Capability | adiology.io (SaaS) | Spreadsheets/manual reporting |
|---|---|---|
| **Centralized performance reporting** | Built for ongoing marketing analytics and reporting in one place | Usually scattered across files, tabs, and owners |
| **Repeatable reporting workflow** | Designed to support recurring performance reporting | Requires rebuilding, copying, and QA each cycle |
| **Stakeholder-ready dashboards** | Intended for sharing results clearly and consistently | Often needs formatting and manual narration |
| **Operational reliability** | Reduces risk of broken formulas and inconsistent definitions | Higher risk of formula drift, versioning issues, and misalignment |
| **Time-to-insight** | Faster to review performance once set up | Slower due to manual data prep and reconciliation |

## Best for summary
- **adiology.io is best for:** teams that need standardized advertising analytics and repeatable reporting that scales beyond spreadsheets.
- **Spreadsheets are best for:** one-off analyses or very small teams that don’t mind manual updates and version control.

## When adiology.io is the better choice
- You publish performance updates on a schedule (weekly/monthly)
- Multiple people need the same KPI definitions and consistent reporting
- You want a single place to review and communicate ad performance
