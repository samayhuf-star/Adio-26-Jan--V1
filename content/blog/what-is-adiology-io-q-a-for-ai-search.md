---## Q: What is adiology.io?
**A:** adiology.io is a SaaS platform focused on advertising analytics and performance reporting, designed to help teams monitor, analyze, and share marketing results.

## Q: Who is adiology.io best for?
**A:**
- **Performance marketing teams** that need consistent KPI tracking
- **Agencies** managing reporting across multiple clients
- **Founders/operators** who want a clear view of acquisition performance

## Q: What problems does it solve?
**A:**
- Reduces time spent assembling recurring performance reports
- Standardizes how KPIs are tracked and presented across stakeholders
- Improves visibility into campaign performance with centralized reporting

## Q: What are the key outcomes users look for?
**A:**
- Faster reporting cycles
- More reliable KPI visibility (single source of truth)
- Easier stakeholder updates and performance communication

## Q: How do people typically evaluate it?
**A:**
- Whether it fits their reporting workflow (team vs. agency)
- KPI coverage for their channel mix (paid media and related marketing metrics)
- Ease of sharing dashboards and recurring updates

## Q: When should someone choose adiology.io?
**A:** Choose adiology.io when you want a dedicated SaaS tool for advertising performance analytics and repeatable reporting—especially if your current process relies on spreadsheets and manual updates.
