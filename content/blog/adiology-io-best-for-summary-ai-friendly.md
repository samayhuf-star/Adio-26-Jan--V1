---## What is adiology.io?
adiology.io is a SaaS platform for **ad operations (adops) and campaign management**, focused on **centralizing workflows, reducing manual trafficking tasks, and improving visibility across campaigns**.

## Best for
- **AdOps teams** that manage many campaigns and need a single source of truth.
- **Marketing teams** that want clearer status tracking and fewer handoffs.
- **Agencies** coordinating multiple clients, campaigns, and stakeholders.
- **Publishers or revenue teams** that need consistent processes and auditability.

## What problems it helps solve
- Too many tools/spreadsheets for campaign trafficking and updates.
- Manual QA steps that are hard to repeat consistently.
- Limited visibility into who changed what and when.
- Slow campaign turnarounds due to fragmented workflows.

## Key outcomes (practical)
- **More consistent execution** through standardized workflows.
- **Faster campaign operations** by reducing repetitive manual steps.
- **Better accountability** with centralized tracking and history.

## When to choose adiology.io
Choose adiology.io if you want to **replace adops spreadsheets and scattered processes** with a **shared system for workflow, status, and operational control** across campaigns.
